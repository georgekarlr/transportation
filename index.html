📖 Roadwise User Manual

1. Finding the Best Route

Type your destination in the search bar (with live autocomplete powered by OpenStreetMap Nominatim), or tap any of the Quick Destination chips (Airport, Market, Hospital, Mall, etc.). You can also click the 📍 pin button and click anywhere on the map to set a target point.

2. Comparing Alternative Routes

Roadwise calculates both the Primary Fastest Route and Alternative Routes (via bypass or coastal highways). Click on any route card or polyline on the map to inspect its distance, estimated travel duration, and traffic congestion level.

3. Travel Modes

Switch between Driving (🚗), Motorcycle / Habal-habal (🏍️), Cab / Taxi (🚕), Bicycle (🚲), and Walking (🚶) to recalculate the optimal route geometry, travel speeds, and estimated transport fares.

4. Live Navigation Simulation

Tap Start Navigation to launch Google Maps-style live turn-by-turn guidance with animated vehicle tracking along the route, upcoming maneuver banners, real-time speedometers, progress bars, and 1x/2x/4x simulation speed options.

5. Booking Rides & Reporting Hazards

Choose Book Ride to request a Motorcycle (Habal-habal) or Cab with estimated metered fares and live driver dispatch. While on a trip, report traffic jams, accidents, and road hazards with a single tap to alert other motorists.
